## What is rettX?

**rettX** is the patient registry of
[Rett Syndrome Europe](https://www.rettsyndrome.eu) — a European
network of Rett Syndrome associations. The registry collects, with
consent, structured information about people living with Rett
Syndrome. Its purpose is to support families and build a better
understanding of the condition.

Today, caregivers maintain records and RSE administrators operate
the registry. Clinician access is planned, not available today.
Data will not be shared with researchers or partners until the
registry's data governance process is in place.

This engineering site explains how rettX software works, how the registry
protects the data entrusted to it, and how contributors develop it. The
[initiative home](https://docs.rettx.eu) explains the wider initiative's
direction and governance.

The patient-facing public site lives at
[**rettx.eu**](https://www.rettx.eu). The caregiver app lives at
[**app.rettx.eu**](https://app.rettx.eu).

## Three things to know

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Patient-first" icon="heart">
    rettX is built around people living with Rett Syndrome and their
    families, with consent, dignity, and accessible experiences at its core.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Access is limited" icon="approve-check">
    Caregiver access is scoped to their records. The admin tool is
    available only to RSE administrators and runs locally; it is not
    publicly deployed or available to clinicians.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Understandable by design" icon="open-book">
    Public documentation explains the architecture and security posture.
    Development principles, specifications, and historical decisions are
    kept separately under **For contributors**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
