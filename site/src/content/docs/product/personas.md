---
title: Who rettX is for
description: The people rettX serves today and the ones it is planned to serve.
---

rettX is built around a small set of people, each with a clear job and only the
access that job needs. This page distinguishes the people who can use the
registry today from access that is still in design or planned.

## Available today

**Caregiver.** Usually a parent or guardian. The caregiver registers the person
with Rett syndrome, uploads diagnosis documents, answers requests for more
information, and reads messages from the registry. The caregiver acts for the
patient; rettX has no separate patient account.

**RSE administrator.** A registry operator from Rett Syndrome Europe (RSE).
The admin tool runs locally and is not publicly deployed. It is available
only to RSE administrators, not clinicians. Administrators confirm that the
uploaded documents belong to the registered person and that they support a Rett
syndrome diagnosis, validate genetic variants, and keep the registry running.
Their permissions are set by role, so the administrator role can be split into
narrower roles as the registry grows.

## In design

**Expert reviewer.** Some diagnoses need a specialist's view, for example when a
genetic variant is complex. An expert reviewer receives only the parts of a
document that matter for a specific question, never the person's name or date of
birth, and answers that question.

## Planned

**Second caregiver.** Another family member or carer who contributes to a
person's record, with narrower access than the primary caregiver.

**Clinician.** A medical professional with access to the people they care for,
scoped by permission. This access is planned; clinicians do not currently
have access to the admin tool.

**Support and operations.** Staff who help caregivers with day-to-day questions
without full administrator access.

**Researcher or partner.** rettX will not share data with researchers or
partners until its data governance process is in place.
